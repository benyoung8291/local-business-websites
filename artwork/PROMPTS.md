# Illustration prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Original transparent PNGs are saved locally in `artwork/`; optimized, alpha-preserving WebP assets used by the website are in `dist/assets/`.

Design reference: https://mindmarket.com/ — bright green and cream, rounded panels, expressive editorial characters, playful connecting paths. No reference artwork or branding was copied.

## team.webp

Use case: illustration-story. Create an original premium editorial cartoon illustration for Ben Young's Melbourne small-business AI consulting website, in the spirit of playful independent creative agencies. Transparent background, wide landscape composition, no text or logos.
Three expressive human characters collaboratively untangling work: left a tradesperson in lemon yellow workwear sitting on a large coral toolbox with laptop; centre a smiling dark-haired person in oversized lavender sweater, peach skin, holding a long cornflower-blue cable in an exaggerated large hand; right a dark-skinned person in coral top and cream trousers leaning on a large simple rounded blue computer showing a single cream checkmark. Cable loosely connects laptop and computer in a graceful loop. Dynamic poses, oversized shoes and hands, asymmetrical silhouettes, tiny expressive facial features, fine charcoal detail lines, hand-drawn but extremely clean flat vector-like cut paper shapes. Palette exclusively lemon yellow #ffe877, periwinkle blue #6489ef, lavender #c8b5e8, coral #ed785d, cream #fff9ec, charcoal #232820, peach and brown skin. No green fills because illustration will be placed on bright apple-green background. Light sophisticated illustration like a contemporary European magazine, funny small details, strong readable silhouettes. Subjects arranged as one connected group across lower horizontal band, fully visible without cropping, balanced negative space around, no shadows no gradients no 3D no texture no UI mockup, no stock corporate blob people. Actual transparent alpha background.

## toolbox.webp
