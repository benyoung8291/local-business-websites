§ 03 · Method

An honest engagement.
Not a sales pitch.

Initial project, then a retainer that scales with what you actually need. If I can't solve it, you don't pay. The process is intentionally short on theatre.

  1. 01
    Discovery ~ 3–5 days

    Diagnosis, not demo-ware.

    A few days inside your business — your data, your workflows, your team. No jargon, no upselling. An honest look at what's actually broken and whether I'm the right person to fix it.

  2. 02
    Build ~ 4–8 weeks

    Build only what earns its keep.

    Custom app, automation, AI integration, or the right combination. I build what your business actually needs — not what fits inside a templated package someone else is selling.

  3. 03
    Partner monthly retainer

    Tech that ages with the business.

    After the build, most clients move onto a monthly retainer. Dial it up when you need more, scale it back when you don't. Your tech ages with the business, not against it.

§ 04 · Colophon

Not an agency.
One operator who likes hard problems.

I'm based in Melbourne and work with clients across Australia — Sydney, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide and everywhere in between. Some clients call me a tech guru; I think of it as being the operator who actually fixes things.

Most consultants will sell you a stack before they understand your business. I'd rather sit down with your team for a few days, learn how you actually work, and only then decide what to build.

The problems I love are the ones that have been on the whiteboard for months. The "we tried that SaaS but it didn't quite fit" problems. The disconnected-systems-and-spreadsheets problems. The ones everyone's accepted as the cost of doing business.

When I see a problem solved often enough, I productise it. fromtheoffice.io is one of those — a rapid-quoting tool for small service businesses. Built once, useful many times.

If I can't solve your problem, there is no fee. I'm not in the business of over-promising.
